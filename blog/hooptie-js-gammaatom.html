Hooptie J's GammaAtom

Jul 30, 2017

A RotorX Atom 83 Mini build. 2" of absolute insanity.

I expected a "slightly better than tinywhoop" — I got a 2" that will outrun my 4".

Frame
RotorX Atom Mini 83mm
Motors
AirbladeUAV 1104/10,000kv
ESCs
4-in-1 Cicada 10A
Cam/VTX combo
HC480 Eachine AIO 25mw 48ch
FC
Hobbyking Omnibus F3 Nano
Battery
450mah 2c 35c

I finally got to put a few packs through it today and managed to pop the camera loose — so I may have to 3D print something (instead of the front standoffs) because it needs a ton more camera angle as well as some additional support up there.

Special thanks to AirBladeUAV.com for the extra stickers, and the great deal on motors and frame!
